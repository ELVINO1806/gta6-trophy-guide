    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Seven stages, in the order that wastes the least rework. Story first, the one missable early, collectibles swept area by area, and the money grind last.

      Missables & Warnings

      One trophy is reported as possibly missable. A few more have windows tied to story progress — start them when they appear.

      How the warnings were decided

      GTABase and PowerPyx both flag Positive Mental Attitude as the only trophy that might be missable. TechWiser adds that the stranger mission chains behind five hidden trophies have windows linked to story progress. Every other trophy can be earned after the story, in free roam.
