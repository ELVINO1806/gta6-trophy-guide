Follow the stages in order. Story first, then side content, then the long grinds last, when free roam and fast travel are fully open.

Missables — read this first

Only 1 trophy in GTA 6 can be missed. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story ends. Do the missable one in Chapter 1 and the platinum is safe.

How not to miss it

  1. Do not finish Chapter 1 until the counter reads 18 / 18.
  2. Meditation spots glow on the map when you walk near one.
  3. Work the map region by region so no spot is left behind.
  4. The tracker above counts your spots — the stepper saves to this browser.
