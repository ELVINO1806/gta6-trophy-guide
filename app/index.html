Roadmap to the Platinum

Seven stages, in the order that wastes the least replay time. Stage 1 comes first for a reason: it holds the guide's only missable trophy.

  1. Stage 1 — Chapter 1 first. Play Gone Fishing and finish Chapter 1. Meditate at all 18 spots before the chapter closes, or Positive Mental Attitude is gone for this playthrough.
  2. Stage 2 — Story missions. Finish Chapters 2, 3, and 4, the named missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory), and the final mission.
  3. Stage 3 — Character strands. Close out Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, and Tyrone, then finish 8 stranger strands across Leonida.
  4. Stage 4 — Activities. Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win Top Fun drone races at all 14 locations, win 20 different races, beat Cal at fishing, finish an arcade game, and bowl a full game.
  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles and exploration. Visit all 52 areas, discover 100 vehicles, complete 3 card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, buy all 8 garages, and register 10 vehicles with Scooter Bros.
  6. Stage 6 — Combat and wanted. Five downs in one Combat Focus, a Rhino encounter, a clean jewelry store robbery, a 4-star escape with your partner, 3 shops in a row, 5 bank vaults, an armored truck, one illegal operation per gang, and a melee drive-by.
  7. Stage 7 — Money and cleanup. Launder $250,000 of marked bills, spend $100,000 in shops, land 250 stunts, finish 50 challenges, make 500 choices, and reach 100% completion. The platinum pops with the last one.

Missables — read this first

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots).

This is the only missable trophy in the set. All 18 meditation spots must be visited during Chapter 1. Once the story moves past Chapter 1, missed spots cannot be revisited on that playthrough, so clear them before finishing the chapter.
